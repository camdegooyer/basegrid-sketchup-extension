## T-moulding floor transition profile

**ID:** `AU-FF-T-MOULDING-TRANSITION-PROFILE`  
**Category:** floor transition components  
**Status:** accepted

A T-shaped linear cover profile spanning a narrow joint between floor finishes of similar height while allowing documented edge or movement clearance below its cap.

Accepted search terms: T-bar floor trim.

Main functions: Cover adjacent finish edges; Bridge a narrow same-level junction; Permit selected floating-floor movement where the profile system allows.
