# Australian timber framing glossary

Generated from the canonical ontology export. Definitions are original plain-English summaries, not reproduced standards text.

## bearer

**ID:** `AU-TF-BEARER`  
**Category:** floor framing  
**Status:** accepted

A horizontal primary floor member supported by posts, stumps, piers, walls or foundations and carrying floor joists above.

Main functions: Support floor joists; Transfer floor loads to the supporting structure below.

## bottom plate

**ID:** `AU-TF-BOTTOM-PLATE`  
**Category:** wall framing  
**Status:** accepted

The lowest horizontal timber member of a wall frame, fixed to the floor or supporting structure beneath it.

Accepted search terms: sole plate.

Main functions: Tie the bottoms of studs together; Locate and fix the wall frame; Transfer wall loads to the structure below.

## ceiling joist

**ID:** `AU-TF-CEILING-JOIST`  
**Category:** conventional roof framing  
**Status:** accepted

A horizontal roof-space member that supports the ceiling and, in a coupled roof, ties the lower ends of opposing rafters.

Main functions: Support ceiling loads; Tie opposing walls or rafter feet where the roof arrangement relies on that action.

## collar tie

**ID:** `AU-TF-COLLAR-TIE`  
**Category:** conventional roof framing  
**Status:** accepted

A horizontal timber member fixed between a pair of opposing rafters above the wall plates.

Accepted search terms: collar.

Main functions: Tie opposing rafters together; Help restrain rafter spread or separation according to the roof arrangement.

## common rafter

**ID:** `AU-TF-COMMON-RAFTER`  
**Category:** conventional roof framing  
**Status:** accepted

A repeated sloping roof member running from a wall plate or eaves support towards the ridge.

Accepted search terms: rafter.

Main functions: Support roof battens and covering; Transfer roof loads to walls, ridge support and any intermediate underpurlins.

## common stud

**ID:** `AU-TF-COMMON-STUD`  
**Category:** wall framing  
**Status:** accepted

A full-height vertical wall member placed at regular centres away from the specialised framing at openings and intersections.

Accepted search terms: wall stud.

Main functions: Support the top plate; Transfer vertical loads to the bottom plate; Provide fixing for wall linings and claddings.

## conventional timber roof frame

**ID:** `AU-TF-CONVENTIONAL-ROOF-FRAME`  
**Category:** conventional roof framing  
**Status:** accepted

A roof structure built on site from individual rafters, ceiling joists, beams, struts, underpurlins, ties and ridge members rather than prefabricated trusses.

Accepted search terms: coupled roof frame.

Main functions: Support the roof covering and ceiling; Transfer roof loads to walls and other supports; Tie and brace the roof into a stable structure.

## creeper truss

**ID:** `AU-TF-CREEPER-TRUSS`  
**Category:** nailplated roof trusses  
**Status:** accepted

A small or shortened truss used near a hipped roof end to complete the roof surface between larger trusses and the end framing.

Main functions: Support roof loads near the hipped end; Complete the stepped truss layout at the diminishing roof width.

## floor joist

**ID:** `AU-TF-FLOOR-JOIST`  
**Category:** floor framing  
**Status:** accepted

A repeated horizontal floor member that spans between supports and carries flooring and floor loads.

Accepted search terms: joist.

Main functions: Support the floor surface and imposed loads; Transfer those loads to bearers, walls or beams.

## floor joist solid blocking

**ID:** `AU-TF-SOLID-BLOCKING`  
**Category:** floor framing  
**Status:** accepted

Short solid pieces fitted tightly between adjacent floor joists to restrain them and help distribute local effects.

Accepted search terms: solid blocking.

Main functions: Restrain deep joists from rolling or twisting; Maintain joist spacing and alignment.

## hanging beam

**ID:** `AU-TF-HANGING-BEAM`  
**Category:** conventional roof framing  
**Status:** accepted

A roof-space beam placed above and generally at right angles to ceiling joists so the joists can be suspended from it and have a shorter effective span.

Main functions: Support ceiling joists from above; Reduce the effective span of ceiling joists.

## head trimmer

**ID:** `AU-TF-HEAD-TRIMMER`  
**Category:** wall framing  
**Status:** accepted pending review

A horizontal framing member forming the top edge of a non-loadbearing wall opening where a structural lintel is not performing that role.

Main functions: Form the upper boundary of a rough opening; Provide fixing and support for short framing above the opening.

## hip rafter

**ID:** `AU-TF-HIP-RAFTER`  
**Category:** conventional roof framing  
**Status:** accepted pending review

A diagonal sloping rafter forming the external intersection of two roof planes and receiving shortened rafters from both sides.

Main functions: Form and support a roof hip; Receive jack rafters from adjoining roof planes.

## hip truss

**ID:** `AU-TF-HIP-TRUSS`  
**Category:** nailplated roof trusses  
**Status:** accepted

A truss used in a prefabricated hip-roof set to form or support the sloping hip portion of the roof.

Main functions: Form part of the hipped roof geometry; Support roof framing and covering in the hip zone.

## jack rafter

**ID:** `AU-TF-JACK-RAFTER`  
**Category:** conventional roof framing  
**Status:** accepted

A shortened sloping rafter that runs between a wall plate and a hip rafter, or between a ridge and a valley rafter.

Main functions: Support roof battens and covering in a hip or valley area; Transfer roof loads to the hip or valley member and other support.

## jack stud

**ID:** `AU-TF-JACK-STUD`  
**Category:** wall framing  
**Status:** accepted

A shortened vertical stud installed above or below a wall opening between a plate and an opening trimmer or lintel.

Main functions: Maintain the wall stud layout around an opening; Support or connect the framing immediately above or below the opening.

## jack truss

**ID:** `AU-TF-JACK-TRUSS`  
**Category:** nailplated roof trusses  
**Status:** accepted

A shortened roof truss in a hip-roof set that is supported at one end by a girder truss or associated hip framing.

Main functions: Support roof loads in the hip zone; Transfer loads to the girder truss and external support.

## jamb stud

**ID:** `AU-TF-JAMB-STUD`  
**Category:** wall framing  
**Status:** accepted

A full-height vertical stud forming one side of a framed door or window opening.

Main functions: Form the side boundary of an opening; Support opening members and provide fixing for the door or window assembly.

## lintel

**ID:** `AU-TF-LINTEL`  
**Category:** wall framing  
**Status:** accepted

A horizontal structural member spanning over a door, window or other wall opening.

Main functions: Carry wall, floor or roof loads over an opening; Transfer those loads to supporting framing at each side.

## nailplated timber roof truss

**ID:** `AU-TF-NAILPLATED-ROOF-TRUSS`  
**Category:** nailplated roof trusses  
**Status:** accepted

A prefabricated triangulated timber roof unit whose chords and webs are joined at nodes, commonly with pressed metal nailplates.

Accepted search terms: roof truss.

Main functions: Span between supports; Support roof and ceiling loads; Transfer loads through triangulated members to supporting walls or beams.

## nogging

**ID:** `AU-TF-NOGGING`  
**Category:** wall framing  
**Status:** accepted

A short horizontal timber member fitted between adjacent wall studs, normally in one or more rows.

Accepted search terms: noggin.

Main functions: Provide lateral restraint to studs; Help keep studs aligned; Provide fixing where required.

## ridgeboard

**ID:** `AU-TF-RIDGEBOARD`  
**Category:** conventional roof framing  
**Status:** accepted

A longitudinal board at the roof ridge used to locate, align and connect the upper ends of opposing rafters.

Accepted search terms: ridge board.

Main functions: Provide a common fixing line for rafter tops; Keep opposing rafter ends aligned during framing.

## roof batten

**ID:** `AU-TF-ROOF-BATTEN`  
**Category:** roof framing common  
**Status:** accepted

A small repeated member fixed across rafters or truss top chords to support and secure the roof covering.

Accepted search terms: batten.

Main functions: Support roof tiles or sheet roofing; Transfer roof-covering loads and wind forces to rafters or trusses.

## roof strut

**ID:** `AU-TF-ROOF-STRUT`  
**Category:** conventional roof framing  
**Status:** accepted

A compression member installed between an underpurlin and a strutting beam or loadbearing wall to carry roof loads downward.

Accepted search terms: strut.

Main functions: Support an underpurlin; Transfer roof loads to a suitable bearing point below.

## roof truss brace

**ID:** `AU-TF-TRUSS-BRACE`  
**Category:** nailplated roof trusses  
**Status:** accepted

A permanent diagonal or longitudinal member fixed across a run of roof trusses to stabilise them as a group and transfer restraint forces.

Accepted search terms: truss bracing.

Main functions: Restrain trusses against movement out of their plane; Transfer stability forces to braced points in the building.

## sill trimmer

**ID:** `AU-TF-SILL-TRIMMER`  
**Category:** wall framing  
**Status:** accepted

A horizontal member forming the bottom edge of a framed window opening between its jamb studs.

Accepted search terms: sill plate.

Main functions: Form the lower boundary of the rough opening; Support short studs and provide fixing below a window.

## strutting beam

**ID:** `AU-TF-STRUTTING-BEAM`  
**Category:** conventional roof framing  
**Status:** accepted

A roof-space beam designed to receive roof struts and transfer their concentrated loads to loadbearing supports without loading the ceiling joists below.

Main functions: Support roof struts; Transfer roof loads to loadbearing walls or other designed supports.

## timber floor frame

**ID:** `AU-TF-FLOOR-FRAME`  
**Category:** floor framing  
**Status:** accepted

The supporting timber skeleton below a floor surface, commonly including bearers, joists, trimming members and blocking.

Main functions: Support floor loads; Transfer floor loads to walls, posts, piers or foundations; Provide a fixing base for flooring.

## timber wall frame

**ID:** `AU-TF-WALL-FRAME`  
**Category:** wall framing  
**Status:** accepted

The structural timber skeleton of a wall, assembled from plates, studs, opening members, noggings and any required bracing.

Main functions: Carry vertical loads where the wall is loadbearing; Provide a stable base for linings, claddings, openings and insulation; Transfer bracing forces to the structure below.

## top plate

**ID:** `AU-TF-TOP-PLATE`  
**Category:** wall framing  
**Status:** accepted

A horizontal timber member fixed along the top of a wall frame, including across framed openings.

Main functions: Tie the tops of studs together; Distribute loads from rafters, trusses or upper framing into the wall.

## trimming joist

**ID:** `AU-TF-TRIMMING-JOIST`  
**Category:** floor framing  
**Status:** accepted

A joist beside a floor opening that receives the ends of one or more cut joists or trimmers and carries their loads to supports.

Main functions: Form a structural side of a floor opening; Support members interrupted by the opening.

## truncated girder truss

**ID:** `AU-TF-TRUNCATED-GIRDER-TRUSS`  
**Category:** nailplated roof trusses  
**Status:** accepted

A shortened flat-topped girder truss in a hip-roof truss system that carries loads from supported hip and jack trusses.

Accepted search terms: truncated girder.

Main functions: Provide the main support line in a hip-truss set; Receive concentrated loads from supported trusses.

## truss bottom chord

**ID:** `AU-TF-TRUSS-BOTTOM-CHORD`  
**Category:** nailplated roof trusses  
**Status:** accepted

The lower outer member of a roof truss, commonly horizontal, connecting the truss heels and supporting ceiling loads where designed.

Accepted search terms: bottom chord.

Main functions: Complete the lower side of the truss triangle; Carry designed tension and ceiling loads.

## truss nailplate

**ID:** `AU-TF-NAILPLATE`  
**Category:** nailplated roof trusses  
**Status:** accepted

A toothed sheet-steel connector pressed into the faces of timber members at a truss node to join them together.

Accepted search terms: nail plate, connector plate.

Main functions: Connect timber truss members at a node; Transfer designed forces between the connected members.

## truss top chord

**ID:** `AU-TF-TRUSS-TOP-CHORD`  
**Category:** nailplated roof trusses  
**Status:** accepted

A sloping outer member forming the top edge of a roof truss and carrying roof battens and roof loads into the truss nodes.

Accepted search terms: top chord.

Main functions: Support roof battens and covering; Carry compression or combined forces between truss nodes.

## truss web

**ID:** `AU-TF-TRUSS-WEB`  
**Category:** nailplated roof trusses  
**Status:** accepted

An internal timber member connecting truss chords at nodes and dividing the truss into stable triangular panels.

Accepted search terms: web member.

Main functions: Transfer forces between top and bottom chords; Maintain the triangulated truss form.

## truss web tie

**ID:** `AU-TF-TRUSS-WEB-TIE`  
**Category:** nailplated roof trusses  
**Status:** accepted

A longitudinal timber restraint fixed across corresponding web members in a run of roof trusses.

Accepted search terms: web tie.

Main functions: Restrain selected truss webs laterally; Link the restraint of one truss to the braced roof system.

## underpurlin

**ID:** `AU-TF-UNDERPURLIN`  
**Category:** conventional roof framing  
**Status:** accepted

A horizontal or near-horizontal roof member placed under and across rafters to give them intermediate support.

Accepted search terms: under purlin.

Main functions: Support rafters between ridge and wall plate; Transfer rafter loads to struts, walls or other designed supports.

## wall brace

**ID:** `AU-TF-WALL-BRACE`  
**Category:** wall framing  
**Status:** accepted

A diagonal strap, timber member or structural sheet arrangement fixed to a wall frame to resist sideways forces.

Accepted search terms: wall bracing.

Main functions: Keep the wall frame square and stable; Transfer racking forces caused by wind and other actions.
