# Australian roof drainage glossary

Generated from the canonical ontology export. Definitions are original plain-English summaries, not reproduced standards text.

## box gutter

**ID:** `AU-RD-BOX-GUTTER`  
**Category:** gutters  
**Status:** accepted

A generally rectangular, internally located gutter with a defined sole, sides, depth and fall, forming the main channel of a box gutter system.

Accepted search terms: internal box gutter.

Main functions: Collect water between internal roof boundaries; Carry water to a sump or outlet; Contain design flow while maintaining required freeboard and overflow.

## box gutter sump

**ID:** `AU-RD-BOX-GUTTER-SUMP`  
**Category:** gutter outlets  
**Status:** accepted

A depressed collection box at the discharge end or low point of a box gutter, providing a larger hydraulic transition to the outlet and normally coordinating with an overflow path.

Accepted search terms: gutter sump.

Main functions: Collect water from the box gutter; Provide head and space around the normal outlet; Coordinate normal discharge and independent overflow.

## box gutter system

**ID:** `AU-RD-BOX-GUTTER-SYSTEM`  
**Category:** roof drainage assemblies  
**Status:** accepted

An internal or concealed roof drainage assembly in which a box-section gutter collects water between or beside roof areas and discharges through a sized outlet or sump with an independent overflow path.

Accepted search terms: internal box gutter system.

Main functions: Collect runoff at an internal roof low line; Carry water to a sump or outlet; Provide an overflow route that protects the building if the normal outlet is restricted.

## controlled gutter back gap

**ID:** `AU-RD-CONTROLLED-BACK-GAP`  
**Category:** gutter overflow components  
**Status:** accepted

A continuous designed gap between the back of an eaves gutter and the fascia or building face, kept open by compatible spacers, clips or brackets so excess water can escape behind and below the gutter without entering the building.

Accepted search terms: gutter back gap, controlled gap.

Main functions: Provide a continuous rear overflow path; Prevent the gutter back from sealing against the fascia; Release surcharge below vulnerable building openings or edges.

## controlled gutter front bead

**ID:** `AU-RD-CONTROLLED-FRONT-BEAD`  
**Category:** gutter overflow components  
**Status:** accepted

A deliberately lowered or shaped outer gutter edge that establishes a continuous front overflow crest below the level at which water could flow into the building.

Accepted search terms: low-front gutter bead.

Main functions: Set the overflow crest on the gutter front; Provide continuous outward overflow; Prevent an excessively high front edge from trapping surcharge against the building.

## downpipe

**ID:** `AU-RD-DOWNPIPE`  
**Category:** downpipes  
**Status:** accepted

A primarily vertical pipe that carries roof water from a gutter outlet, rainhead or sump to an underground stormwater drain, tank inlet, spreader or approved discharge point.

Accepted search terms: rainwater downpipe.

Main functions: Convey roof water down the building; Connect roof outlets to the site stormwater system; Keep discharge controlled and clear of the building fabric.

## downpipe bend

**ID:** `AU-RD-DOWNPIPE-BEND`  
**Category:** downpipe fittings  
**Status:** accepted

A shaped fitting that changes the direction of a downpipe run by a stated angle while maintaining a continuous internal waterway.

Accepted search terms: downpipe elbow.

Main functions: Change downpipe direction; Form offsets and lower shoes with compatible fittings; Maintain a closed water path through the change.

## downpipe clip

**ID:** `AU-RD-DOWNPIPE-CLIP`  
**Category:** downpipe supports  
**Status:** accepted

A repeated strap, saddle or proprietary bracket that holds a downpipe to a wall or frame while keeping its route, clearance and joints stable.

Accepted search terms: downpipe bracket, downpipe saddle.

Main functions: Restrain the downpipe to its support; Maintain alignment and wall clearance; Transfer pipe, water and wind actions to the building.

## downpipe joiner

**ID:** `AU-RD-DOWNPIPE-JOINER`  
**Category:** downpipe fittings  
**Status:** accepted

A socket, sleeve or formed connector that joins two downpipe lengths while preserving alignment and a continuous waterway.

Accepted search terms: downpipe socket, downpipe coupling.

Main functions: Connect downpipe lengths; Maintain cross-section alignment; Provide the required lap, socket or sealed joint.

## downpipe offset assembly

**ID:** `AU-RD-DOWNPIPE-OFFSET`  
**Category:** downpipe fittings  
**Status:** accepted

A short dog-leg in a downpipe made from two bends and an intermediate pipe length, used to shift the downpipe axis around an eaves projection or other obstruction.

Accepted search terms: downpipe swan neck.

Main functions: Shift the downpipe between non-aligned upper and lower axes; Clear the eaves, fascia or wall projection; Maintain continuous gravity flow through the route change.

## downpipe shoe

**ID:** `AU-RD-DOWNPIPE-SHOE`  
**Category:** downpipe fittings  
**Status:** accepted

A curved or angled open-ended fitting at the bottom of a downpipe that turns discharge away from the wall onto a lower roof, surface drain or approved open discharge location.

Accepted search terms: downpipe kick.

Main functions: Turn downpipe flow outward at its base; Direct open discharge away from the wall; Reduce uncontrolled wetting at the downpipe termination.

## downpipe spreader

**ID:** `AU-RD-DOWNPIPE-SPREADER`  
**Category:** downpipe fittings  
**Status:** accepted

A horizontal distribution fitting at a downpipe outlet that divides or spreads discharge across a lower roof so the receiving roof covering and drainage system are not loaded at one small point.

Accepted search terms: rainwater spreader.

Main functions: Distribute upper-roof water across a lower roof; Reduce concentrated discharge at one point; Direct flow down the receiving roof toward its drainage edge.

## downpipe-to-stormwater adaptor

**ID:** `AU-RD-DOWNPIPE-STORMWATER-ADAPTOR`  
**Category:** downpipe fittings  
**Status:** accepted

A transition fitting at ground level that connects the shape and size of an above-ground downpipe to the inlet of an underground stormwater drain or tank line.

Accepted search terms: downpipe adaptor.

Main functions: Transition between downpipe and stormwater pipe geometry; Provide a controlled lower connection; Keep the connection accessible or sealed as required by the selected system.

## eaves gutter

**ID:** `AU-RD-EAVES-GUTTER`  
**Category:** gutters  
**Status:** accepted

An external gutter fixed along the lower edge of a roof to receive runoff discharged over the eaves end of the roof covering.

Accepted search terms: external eaves gutter.

Main functions: Catch water at the eaves; Convey water to downpipe outlets; Overflow outward through the selected overflow measure.

## eaves gutter overflow system

**ID:** `AU-RD-GUTTER-OVERFLOW-SYSTEM`  
**Category:** overflow assemblies  
**Status:** accepted

The deliberate set of openings, lowered edges, spacers or dedicated devices that lets excess water escape an eaves gutter outward before it can enter or damage the building.

Accepted search terms: gutter overflow provision.

Main functions: Release excess water when normal outlets are exceeded or blocked; Direct overflow visibly away from the building; Preserve a continuous overflow path along or at nominated parts of the gutter.

## eaves gutter system

**ID:** `AU-RD-EAVES-GUTTER-SYSTEM`  
**Category:** roof drainage assemblies  
**Status:** accepted

A roof-edge drainage assembly comprising an eaves gutter, supports, stop ends, corners and joins where needed, one or more outlets, and the specified continuous or dedicated overflow measures.

Accepted search terms: external gutter system.

Main functions: Collect water leaving the lower roof edge; Carry water to gutter outlets; Keep overflow from entering or damaging the building.

## external gutter corner

**ID:** `AU-RD-GUTTER-EXTERNAL-CORNER`  
**Category:** gutter fittings  
**Status:** accepted

Accepted search terms: external gutter angle.

Main functions: Turn an eaves gutter around an external plan corner; Join matching gutter profiles; Maintain water containment and overflow continuity.

## front-face gutter overflow slot

**ID:** `AU-RD-FRONT-FACE-OVERFLOW-SLOT`  
**Category:** gutter overflow components  
**Status:** accepted

One of a repeated series of purpose-formed openings through the outer face of an eaves gutter, positioned to release excess water outward below the level that could threaten the building.

Accepted search terms: gutter overflow slot.

Main functions: Provide continuous-distribution overflow capacity; Make gutter surcharge visible; Direct overflow through the front rather than behind the gutter.

## gutter back outlet

**ID:** `AU-RD-BACK-OUTLET`  
**Category:** gutter outlets

A gutter outlet passing through the rear wall of an eaves gutter into a rainhead or offset connection rather than dropping directly through the gutter sole.

Accepted search terms: rear gutter outlet.

## gutter bracket
