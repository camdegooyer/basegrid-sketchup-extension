# Australian termite management glossary

Generated from the canonical ontology export. Definitions are original plain-English summaries, not reproduced standards text.

## chemical termite treated zone

**ID:** `AU-TM-CHEMICAL-TREATED-ZONE`  
**Category:** termite barriers  
**Status:** accepted

A defined soil or substrate zone treated with an approved termiticide to reduce termite passage through or activity within that zone.

Accepted search terms: chemical termite barrier, treated soil zone.

Main functions: Create a chemically treated termite-management zone; Protect selected perimeters, under-slab areas or penetrations; Allow replenishment where the system includes reticulation.

## graded stone termite barrier

**ID:** `AU-TM-GRADED-STONE-BARRIER`  
**Category:** termite barriers  
**Status:** accepted

A compact zone of specially graded hard particles sized and installed so termites cannot readily pass between or move the particles.

Accepted search terms: granular termite barrier.

Main functions: Physically obstruct termite passage; Protect selected slab perimeters, penetrations, joints or suspended-floor areas; Form a component of a complete termite management system.

## stainless steel mesh termite barrier

**ID:** `AU-TM-STAINLESS-MESH-BARRIER`  
**Category:** termite barriers  
**Status:** accepted

A fine corrosion-resistant steel mesh fixed and sealed across joints, perimeters or service penetrations so termites cannot pass through the mesh openings.

Accepted search terms: termite mesh.

Main functions: Physically block termite passage; Bridge and seal vulnerable construction interfaces; Form collars or strips within a complete management system.

## termite management durable notice

**ID:** `AU-TM-DURABLE-NOTICE`  
**Category:** termite identification  
**Status:** accepted

A permanently fixed identification notice recording the installed termite management system and information needed for later inspection or maintenance.

Accepted search terms: termite notice.

Main functions: Identify the installed termite management system; Record installation and maintenance information; Provide enduring information to owners, inspectors and trades.

## termite management system

**ID:** `AU-TM-TERMITE-MANAGEMENT-SYSTEM`  
**Category:** termite management  
**Status:** accepted

A coordinated set of physical barriers, treated zones, inspection provisions and identification information used to reduce termite entry risk or make concealed entry more difficult.

Accepted search terms: termite protection system.

Main functions: Reduce the risk of concealed termite entry; Force termite activity into inspectable locations where the selected system relies on detection; Protect susceptible primary building elements as required by the project.

## termite penetration collar

**ID:** `AU-TM-PENETRATION-COLLAR`  
**Category:** termite barriers  
**Status:** accepted

A rigid or flexible barrier component fitted tightly around a service passing through a slab or other protected construction to close a termite entry path.

Accepted search terms: termite pipe collar.

Main functions: Seal the termite-management system around a service penetration; Connect the penetration detail to the surrounding barrier or slab; Prevent concealed passage beside the service.

## termite sheet barrier

**ID:** `AU-TM-SHEET-BARRIER`  
**Category:** termite barriers  
**Status:** accepted

A continuous proprietary sheet material installed across likely termite entry paths at slab perimeters, joints, walls or penetrations as part of a tested system.

Accepted search terms: sheet termite barrier.

Main functions: Block concealed termite passage through the detailed area; Direct termite activity toward an inspectable edge where the system is designed to do so; Join with compatible collars and seals around penetrations.

## termite shield

**ID:** `AU-TM-TERMITE-SHIELD`  
**Category:** termite barriers  
**Status:** accepted

A formed metal sheet placed over a stump, pier or wall support to obstruct concealed termite passage and expose any shelter tubes around its projecting edges.

Accepted search terms: ant cap, termite cap.

Main functions: Interrupt concealed termite access over a support; Force termite shelter tubes into a more visible path; Separate susceptible framing from the support below.

## termite treatment reticulation pipe

**ID:** `AU-TM-RETICULATION-PIPE`  
**Category:** termite treatment  
**Status:** accepted pending review

A perforated pipe network installed in a treatment zone so termiticide can be introduced or replenished without reopening the protected construction.

Accepted search terms: termite reticulation system, termite retic pipe.

Main functions: Distribute termiticide through a selected soil zone; Allow future replenishment of a chemical termite-management component; Connect treatment zones to accessible charging points.
