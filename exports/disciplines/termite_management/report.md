## Objects

- **chemical termite treated zone** (`AU-TM-CHEMICAL-TREATED-ZONE`) — A defined soil or substrate zone treated with an approved termiticide to reduce termite passage through or activity within that zone.
- **graded stone termite barrier** (`AU-TM-GRADED-STONE-BARRIER`) — A compact zone of specially graded hard particles sized and installed so termites cannot readily pass between or move the particles.
- **stainless steel mesh termite barrier** (`AU-TM-STAINLESS-MESH-BARRIER`) — A fine corrosion-resistant steel mesh fixed and sealed across joints, perimeters or service penetrations so termites cannot pass through the mesh openings.
- **termite management durable notice** (`AU-TM-DURABLE-NOTICE`) — A permanently fixed identification notice recording the installed termite management system and information needed for later inspection or maintenance.
- **termite management system** (`AU-TM-TERMITE-MANAGEMENT-SYSTEM`) — A coordinated set of physical barriers, treated zones, inspection provisions and identification information used to reduce termite entry risk or make concealed entry more difficult.
- **termite penetration collar** (`AU-TM-PENETRATION-COLLAR`) — A rigid or flexible barrier component fitted tightly around a service passing through a slab or other protected construction to close a termite entry path.
- **termite sheet barrier** (`AU-TM-SHEET-BARRIER`) — A continuous proprietary sheet material installed across likely termite entry paths at slab perimeters, joints, walls or penetrations as part of a tested system.
- **termite shield** (`AU-TM-TERMITE-SHIELD`) — A formed metal sheet placed over a stump, pier or wall support to obstruct concealed termite passage and expose any shelter tubes around its projecting edges.
- **termite treatment reticulation pipe** (`AU-TM-RETICULATION-PIPE`) — A perforated pipe network installed in a treatment zone so termiticide can be introduced or replenished without reopening the protected construction.
