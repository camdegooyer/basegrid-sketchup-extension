# Australian concrete foundations glossary

Generated from the canonical ontology export. Definitions are original plain-English summaries, not reproduced standards text.

## bar-chair base

**ID:** `AU-CF-CHAIR-BASE`  
**Category:** reinforcement supports  
**Status:** accepted pending review

A flat base fitted below a wire or narrow bar chair to spread its load on soft ground or a plastic membrane and reduce puncture risk.

Accepted search terms: chair plate.

Main functions: Stabilise a bar chair on a soft or flexible surface; Spread chair load; Reduce local damage to the under-slab membrane.

## bulk pier

**ID:** `AU-CF-BULK-PIER`  
**Category:** point supports  
**Status:** accepted pending review

A local deep mass-concrete support below a slab edge beam or footing that carries load through fill or weak near-surface material to suitable natural ground.

Accepted search terms: mass concrete pier.

Main functions: Provide deep local support below a slab or footing; Transfer load through unsuitable shallow material to stronger foundation material.

## concrete formwork

**ID:** `AU-CF-CONCRETE-FORMWORK`  
**Category:** temporary concrete works  
**Status:** accepted pending review

A temporary mould and support system that contains fresh concrete and gives a slab, beam, footing, wall or column its required shape until the concrete can support itself.

Accepted search terms: forms, boxing.

Main functions: Contain fresh concrete; Define member faces, edges, levels and rebates; Support wet concrete and construction loads until stripping.

## concrete reinforcement assembly

**ID:** `AU-CF-REINFORCEMENT-ASSEMBLY`  
**Category:** reinforcement  
**Status:** accepted

The designed arrangement of steel bars, mesh, ligatures, ties and supports fixed in position before concrete is placed.

Accepted search terms: reo.

Main functions: Add tensile and crack-control capacity to concrete members; Keep individual reinforcement pieces in their designed relationship; Maintain reinforcement position during concreting.

## concrete slab panel

**ID:** `AU-CF-CONCRETE-SLAB-PANEL`  
**Category:** slab members  
**Status:** accepted

The relatively thin, broad concrete floor portion between or over the deeper beams of a residential slab foundation.

Accepted search terms: slab panel.

Main functions: Form the floor surface; Span or bear between supporting ribs, beams or ground; Distribute floor loads into the foundation system.

## concrete slab-on-ground assembly

**ID:** `AU-CF-SLAB-ON-GROUND-ASSEMBLY`  
**Category:** concrete slabs  
**Status:** accepted

A concrete floor and foundation assembly cast close to the ground and supported continuously or at its beams by prepared soil or fill.

Accepted search terms: ground slab, slab on grade.

Main functions: Form the ground floor; Transfer building and floor loads into the supporting ground; Provide a base for walls and floor finishes.

## deepened edge beam

**ID:** `AU-CF-DEEPENED-EDGE-BEAM`  
**Category:** slab members  
**Status:** accepted

An edge beam made deeper than the adjoining slab panel so it reaches suitable support and provides greater stiffness or load capacity.

Main functions: Support the slab perimeter and external walls; Reach deeper foundation material or bridge local fill conditions; Increase perimeter stiffness.

## footing slab assembly

**ID:** `AU-CF-FOOTING-SLAB-ASSEMBLY`  
**Category:** concrete slabs  
**Status:** accepted

A ground-floor slab supported by separately poured strip footings or edge beams rather than an integral excavated raft-beam grid.

Accepted search terms: slab footing.

Main functions: Form the floor surface; Transfer floor and wall loads through the slab and separate footing system; Adapt the foundation arrangement to its site and wall construction.

## foundation material

**ID:** `AU-CF-FOUNDATION-MATERIAL`  
**Category:** foundation substrates  
**Status:** accepted

The natural soil, rock or properly prepared material directly supporting a footing, slab beam, pad or pier.

Accepted search terms: foundation soil.

Main functions: Receive loads from the footing system; Provide the bearing substrate beneath the building.

## pad footing

**ID:** `AU-CF-PAD-FOOTING`  
**Category:** footing members  
**Status:** accepted

An isolated concrete footing below a stump, pier, post or column that spreads a concentrated load into the foundation material.

Accepted search terms: concrete pad.

Main functions: Support an individual vertical support; Spread a point load over a larger foundation area.

## reinforcement bar chair

**ID:** `AU-CF-BAR-CHAIR`  
**Category:** reinforcement supports  
**Status:** accepted

A small wire, concrete or plastic support placed beneath or between reinforcement to hold it at the designed level while concrete is poured.

Accepted search terms: bar chair, reo chair.

Main functions: Maintain concrete cover below reinforcement; Prevent reinforcement sagging or moving during concreting; Support mesh, bars or trench mesh at their scheduled elevation.

## reinforcement ligature

**ID:** `AU-CF-LIGATURE`  
**Category:** reinforcement  
**Status:** accepted

A closed or partly closed bent reinforcing bar fitted around longitudinal bars to hold them in position and provide transverse restraint in a beam, column or pier cage.

Accepted search terms: fitment, stirrup.

Main functions: Hold longitudinal bars in their designed arrangement; Provide transverse reinforcement and restraint; Define the shape of a reinforcement cage.

## reinforcement tie wire

**ID:** `AU-CF-TIE-WIRE`  
**Category:** reinforcement supports  
**Status:** accepted pending review

A small flexible wire twisted around intersecting reinforcement to hold bars and mesh in position before and during concrete placement.

Accepted search terms: reo tie wire, binding wire.

Main functions: Temporarily secure reinforcement intersections; Keep bars, mesh and ligatures in their scheduled relationship during placement.

## slab edge beam

**ID:** `AU-CF-EDGE-BEAM`  
**Category:** slab members  
**Status:** accepted

A reinforced concrete beam running around the perimeter of a slab foundation and supporting its outer walls and edge.

Accepted search terms: edge beam.

Main functions: Support perimeter wall and slab-edge loads; Stiffen and define the slab perimeter; Transfer edge loads into the foundation material.

## slab edge insulation

**ID:** `AU-CF-SLAB-EDGE-INSULATION`  
**Category:** slab insulation  
**Status:** accepted

A rigid or protected insulation strip installed against the vertical perimeter edge of a concrete slab to reduce heat flow through the slab edge.

Accepted search terms: perimeter slab insulation.

Main functions: Reduce perimeter heat loss or heat gain; Improve the thermal continuity of the floor envelope.

## slab edge rebate

**ID:** `AU-CF-EDGE-REBATE`  
**Category:** slab features  
**Status:** accepted

A continuous step or recess formed along a concrete slab edge to receive masonry veneer or cavity-wall construction and its drainage details.

Accepted search terms: edge rebate.

Main functions: Provide a lowered bearing ledge for masonry; Create the geometry needed for wall flashing and drainage at the slab edge.

## slab internal beam

**ID:** `AU-CF-INTERNAL-BEAM`  
**Category:** slab members  
**Status:** accepted

A deeper reinforced concrete strip formed within a slab foundation to stiffen the slab and support internal load lines.

Accepted search terms: internal beam, internal thickening.

Main functions: Stiffen the slab foundation; Support internal walls or concentrated load lines; Transfer loads through the slab to the ground.

## slab void former

**ID:** `AU-CF-SLAB-VOID-FORMER`  
**Category:** slab formers  
**Status:** accepted

A lightweight unit or form placed beneath a slab to reserve a void while concrete ribs and the slab panel are cast around or above it.

Accepted search terms: void former.

Main functions: Displace concrete from non-structural zones; Define the spacing and shape of concrete ribs; Remain in place or provide temporary form according to the system.

## steel reinforcing bar

**ID:** `AU-CF-REINFORCING-BAR`  
**Category:** reinforcement  
**Status:** accepted

A straight, bent or coiled steel bar embedded in concrete to carry tensile forces, control cracking or reinforce a local detail.

Accepted search terms: reinforcement bar, reo bar, rebar.

Main functions: Reinforce concrete in a nominated direction; Carry tension and help control cracking; Provide local reinforcement at corners, beams, footings and other details.

## stepped strip footing

**ID:** `AU-CF-STEPPED-STRIP-FOOTING`  
**Category:** footing members  
**Status:** accepted

A strip footing divided into level runs joined by vertical or sloping transitions so it can follow a sloping site while maintaining suitable bearing.

Accepted search terms: stepped footing.

Main functions: Carry a continuous wall load across changing ground levels; Maintain appropriate footing geometry on a slope.

## stiffened raft slab

**ID:** `AU-CF-STIFFENED-RAFT-SLAB`  
**Category:** concrete slabs  
**Status:** accepted

A slab-on-ground cast integrally with deeper edge and internal beams arranged as a stiff structural grid in excavated trenches.

Accepted search terms: stiffened raft.

## strip footing

**ID:** `AU-CF-STRIP-FOOTING`  
**Category:** footing members

Accepted search terms: continuous strip footing.

**ID:** `AU-CF-STRIP-FOOTING-SYSTEM`  
**Category:** footing systems

A connected layout of continuous reinforced concrete footings running beneath loadbearing walls or other line loads.

## structural concrete

**ID:** `AU-CF-CONCRETE-MATERIAL`

**Status:** accepted

A hardened composite material made from cementitious binder, water and aggregate, placed while workable to form slabs, beams, footings and other members.

Accepted search terms: concrete.

Main functions: Form compression-resistant structural members; Encapsulate and work with reinforcement; Take the shape of the excavation or formwork during casting.

## stump and pad footing system

**ID:** `AU-CF-STUMP-PAD-SYSTEM`  
**Category:** footing systems  
**Status:** accepted

A repeated foundation system in which vertical stumps support the floor structure and bear on individual pads or soleplates below.

Accepted search terms: stump footing system.

## subfloor stump

**ID:** `AU-CF-STUMP`  
**Category:** point supports

A short vertical support between an individual footing and a bearer or other suspended-floor member.

Accepted search terms: stump.

Main functions: Support bearers or floor beams above the ground; Transfer floor and wall loads down to a pad footing; Maintain the required subfloor level.

Accepted search terms: TM.
