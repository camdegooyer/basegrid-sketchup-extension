# Australian glazing glossary

Generated from the canonical ontology export. Definitions are original plain-English summaries, not reproduced standards text.

## annealed glass pane

**ID:** `AU-GL-ANNEALED-GLASS`  
**Category:** glass products  
**Status:** accepted

Ordinary glass cooled without the strengthening process used for heat-strengthened or toughened glass, so it retains its normal strength and breakage behaviour.

Accepted search terms: ordinary annealed glass.

Main functions: Provide general glazing where permitted; Serve as feedstock for cutting, coating, laminating or later heat treatment; Break with ordinary annealed-glass behaviour.

## body-tinted glass pane

**ID:** `AU-GL-BODY-TINTED-GLASS`  
**Category:** solar control glass  
**Status:** accepted

Glass coloured throughout its body by ingredients in the glass rather than solely by a surface-applied film or coating.

Accepted search terms: tinted glass, heat-absorbing glass.

Main functions: Reduce or alter transmitted light; Modify solar heat gain and appearance; Serve as a base for laminating, coating or heat treatment.

## building glazing system

A complete arrangement that retains one or more glass panes or manufactured glazing units in a frame, sash, door, channel or other support, including the necessary beads, gaskets, blocks, sealants and joints.

Main functions: Retain glass safely; Transfer glass actions to supporting construction; Control water, air and contact at the glass perimeter.
