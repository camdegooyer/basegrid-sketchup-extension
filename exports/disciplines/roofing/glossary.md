## barge capping tile

**ID:** `AU-RO-BARGE-TILE`  
**Category:** roof tile accessories  
**Status:** accepted

A purpose-made tile or tile fitting used along the sloping verge of a tiled roof to weather and finish the edge beside the barge or gable.

Accepted search terms: barge tile, verge tile.

Main functions: Weather the sloping roof edge; Cover or finish the verge; Provide an individually fixed edge accessory.
