# Australian steel framing glossary

Generated from the canonical ontology export. Definitions are original plain-English summaries, not reproduced standards text.

## built-up steel wall post

**ID:** `AU-SF-WALL-POST`  
**Category:** wall members  
**Status:** accepted

A strengthened vertical wall member made from one heavier or several connected cold-formed sections to carry concentrated loads beyond an ordinary repeated stud role.

Accepted search terms: steel stud post.

Main functions: Carry concentrated vertical loads; Support beam or lintel reactions; Transfer local load through the wall frame.

## cold-formed C-section

**ID:** `AU-SF-C-SECTION`  
**Category:** section forms  
**Status:** accepted

A cold-formed steel section with a web and two flanges forming a C-shaped cross-section, used for studs, joists, rafters, bearers or other framing roles.

Accepted search terms: C section, C-channel.

Main functions: Provide an efficient light-steel framing cross-section; Form members in several structural roles; Receive connections through web and flanges.

## cold-formed steel ceiling batten

**ID:** `AU-SF-CEILING-BATTEN`

**Status:** accepted

A light cold-formed steel member fixed beneath trusses, joists or rafters to provide aligned repeated support for ceiling lining.
