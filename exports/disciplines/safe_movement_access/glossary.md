# Australian safe movement access glossary

Generated from the canonical ontology export. Definitions are original plain-English summaries, not reproduced standards text.

## access-path retaining-wall barrier assembly

**ID:** `AU-SA-RETAINING-WALL-ACCESS-BARRIER-ASSEMBLY`  
**Category:** barrier assemblies  
**Status:** accepted

A barrier installed along a retaining-wall edge where that wall forms part of or directly adjoins a delineated access path to or between buildings.

Accepted search terms: retaining-wall balustrade.

Main functions: Protect the access-path fall edge; Connect barrier anchors to the retaining-wall or adjacent structure; Preserve drainage and wall cap details.

**ID:** `AU-SA-ALPINE-EXTERNAL-ACCESS-ASSEMBLY`

**Status:** accepted
