## beam tee section

**ID:** `AU-SS-BEAM-TEE`  
**Category:** tee sections  
**Status:** accepted

Main functions: Provide a tee-shaped structural profile; Serve members or fabricated parts where scheduled; Provide one flange and a stem for connections.
