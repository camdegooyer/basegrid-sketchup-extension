## bath or spa lip waterstop

**ID:** `AU-WP-BATH-LIP-WATERSTOP`  
**Category:** wet area flashings  
**Status:** accepted

A waterproof angle or upstand installed beneath the lip of an inserted bath or spa so the adjoining waterproof shelf or wall system terminates against the vessel without allowing water behind the surround.

Accepted search terms: bath waterstop.

Main functions: Stop water at the vessel lip; Connect shelf membrane to the bath or spa; Protect the concealed surround below.
