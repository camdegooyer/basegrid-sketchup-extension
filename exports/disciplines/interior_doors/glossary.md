# Australian interior doors glossary

Generated from the canonical ontology export. Definitions are original plain-English summaries, not reproduced standards text.

## blockboard-core flush internal door leaf

**ID:** `AU-ID-BLOCKBOARD-CORE-FLUSH-DOOR-LEAF`  
**Category:** flush door leaves  
**Status:** accepted

A flush internal door leaf whose core is built from closely arranged timber strips or blocks faced with sheet material to form a stiff, relatively dense panel.

Accepted search terms: STPC door.

Main functions: Provide a stiff flush leaf; Give continuous timber-rich support behind the faces; Carry compatible hinges, locks and pulls.

## butt-hinge leaf plate

**ID:** `AU-ID-BUTT-HINGE-LEAF`  
**Category:** hinge components  
**Status:** accepted

One of the two flat plates of a butt hinge, containing screw holes and rolled knuckles and fixed to either the door leaf or the jamb.

Accepted search terms: hinge leaf.

Main functions: Fix one side of the hinge to its substrate; Form alternating knuckles around the pin; Transfer load between screws and the hinge axis.

## butt-hinge pin

**ID:** `AU-ID-BUTT-HINGE-PIN`  
**Category:** hinge components  
**Status:** accepted

The axial metal pin passing through the interleaved knuckles of a butt hinge to connect its two leaves and form the rotation axis.

Accepted search terms: hinge pin.

Main functions: Join the hinge leaves; Define the rotation axis; Carry shear and bearing forces between hinge knuckles.
