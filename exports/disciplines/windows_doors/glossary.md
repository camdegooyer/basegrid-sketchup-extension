# Australian windows doors glossary

Generated from the canonical ontology export. Definitions are original plain-English summaries, not reproduced standards text.

## French-door meeting stile

**ID:** `AU-WD-DOOR-MEETING-STILE`  
**Category:** door panel members  
**Status:** accepted

Either of the central vertical edge members where paired French-door leaves meet and close without a fixed mullion between them.

Accepted search terms: meeting stiles.

Main functions: Close the joint between paired leaves; Receive seals, latch, lock or bolts; Transfer closing and wind actions between active and inactive leaves.

## adjustable louvre window assembly

**ID:** `AU-WD-ADJUSTABLE-LOUVRE-WINDOW`  
**Category:** window assemblies  
**Status:** accepted

A window assembly containing a series of overlapping horizontal or vertical blades that rotate together between open and closed positions to control ventilation.

Accepted search terms: louvre window, jalousie window.

Main functions: Provide adjustable ventilation; Overlap blades to close the opening; Coordinate blades, clips, operating bar, frame and seals as one product.

## awning sash

**ID:** `AU-WD-AWNING-SASH`  
**Category:** window sashes  
**Status:** accepted

A glazed sash that pivots about a horizontal axis at or near its top so its bottom edge projects outward.

Accepted search terms: projected sash.

## awning window assembly

**ID:** `AU-WD-AWNING-WINDOW`

An operable window whose sash is hinged or pivoted about a horizontal axis at or near its top and projects outward at the bottom.
