# Australian site civil glossary

Generated from the canonical ontology export. Definitions are original plain-English summaries, not reproduced standards text.

## clean quarry sand bedding layer

**ID:** `AU-SC-QUARRY-SAND-BEDDING`  
**Category:** earth fill  
**Status:** accepted

A level layer of clean quarry sand placed over supporting fill to provide an even surface beneath the under-slab layers.

Accepted search terms: sand blinding, bedding sand.

Main functions: Level and smooth the prepared fill surface; Provide a bedding surface below the slab membrane; Reduce sharp contact points beneath flexible underlay.

## controlled fill

**ID:** `AU-SC-CONTROLLED-FILL`  
**Category:** earth fill  
**Status:** accepted

Selected soil or sand placed in controlled layers and mechanically compacted to form a documented supporting layer beneath or beside construction.

Accepted search terms: engineered fill.

Main functions: Raise or shape the building platform; Provide prepared support below slabs and edge beams where permitted; Replace unsuitable or excavated material with compacted material.

## drainage sump

**ID:** `AU-SC-SUMP`  
**Category:** site drainage  
**Status:** accepted

A low chamber or pit that receives drainage water for gravity discharge, storage or pumping to a suitable outlet.

Accepted search terms: sump pit.

Main functions: Collect water at a low point; Provide storage below connected drain inverts; Permit water to discharge or be pumped onward.

## prepared building platform

**ID:** `AU-SC-PREPARED-BUILDING-PLATFORM`  
**Category:** site preparation  
**Status:** accepted

The shaped and compacted ground area prepared to receive a building's footing or slab system and direct surface water appropriately.

Accepted search terms: building pad.

Main functions: Provide a stable working and founding area; Establish the required site levels below the building; Support site drainage away from the building.

## rolled fill

**ID:** `AU-SC-ROLLED-FILL`  
**Category:** earth fill  
**Status:** accepted

Sand or clay fill placed in layers and compacted by repeated rolling with suitable mechanical equipment under the Housing Provisions method.

Main functions: Raise and level the slab-support area; Provide compacted fill below construction within its permitted use.

## silt pit

**ID:** `AU-SC-SILT-PIT`  
**Category:** site drainage  
**Status:** accepted

An accessible underground chamber that receives drainage water, allows heavier sediment to settle below the outlet and connects to a downstream drain.

Accepted search terms: silt trap.

Main functions: Collect sediment before water enters the downstream line; Provide a transition between drain inlets and outlet; Allow cleaning and maintenance.

## soaker well

**ID:** `AU-SC-SOAKER-WELL`  
**Category:** site drainage  
**Status:** accepted

An underground chamber that receives stormwater and allows it to soak into suitable surrounding soil as an on-site disposal method.

Accepted search terms: soakwell.

Main functions: Temporarily store incoming stormwater; Disperse water into permeable surrounding ground; Reduce direct discharge from the site where approved.

## stormwater detention or retention tank

**ID:** `AU-SC-STORMWATER-TANK`  
**Category:** site drainage  
**Status:** accepted pending review

A tank in a stormwater system that temporarily stores collected runoff for controlled release, reuse or retention on the property.

Accepted search terms: stormwater tank.

Main functions: Receive and store stormwater; Limit or delay discharge according to the system design; Provide water for reuse where configured.

## subsoil drain

**ID:** `AU-SC-SUBSOIL-DRAIN`  
**Category:** site drainage  
**Status:** accepted

A graded underground drainage run, commonly using perforated pipe and surrounding filter material, that collects water from the soil.

Accepted search terms: sub-soil drain, ag drain, agricultural drain.

Main functions: Admit subsurface water along its run; Carry collected water to a pit, sump or outlet.

## subsoil drainage system

**ID:** `AU-SC-SUBSOIL-DRAINAGE-SYSTEM`  
**Category:** site drainage  
**Status:** accepted

An underground drainage assembly that collects subsurface water and conveys it away from footings, basement walls, retaining walls or the area beneath a building.

Accepted search terms: sub-soil drainage system.

Main functions: Intercept and collect subsurface water; Reduce water build-up beside below-ground construction; Discharge collected water to a maintained drainage point.

## underground stormwater drain

**ID:** `AU-SC-STORMWATER-DRAIN`  
**Category:** site drainage  
**Status:** accepted

A buried solid-wall pipe and fitting run that conveys roof or site stormwater to an approved discharge or on-site system.

Accepted search terms: stormwater pipe.

Main functions: Carry stormwater below ground; Connect pits, downpipes and on-site stormwater components; Discharge water without flowing back into the building.

## unretained fill embankment

**ID:** `AU-SC-FILL-EMBANKMENT`  
**Category:** earthworks  
**Status:** accepted pending review

A shaped mass of placed and compacted earth that raises the site and is held by its own stable side slope rather than a retaining wall.

Accepted search terms: fill batter.

Main functions: Raise or reshape site levels; Create a stable sloping edge to a fill area; Support landscape or building-platform areas where permitted.
